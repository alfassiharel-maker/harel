VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
HYPOTHESIS SUPPORTED in a band: bit-exact, fewer ops, and lower wall-clock for sparsity in [0.004, 0.500].
  The op-count reduction is arithmetic and exact; the wall-clock win is the load-bearing part and it is present here on equal substrate.

Bounds established by the controls, all measured:
  - dense delta: CCP loses (no exploitable sparsity) — as it must.
  - single input / no reuse: CCP loses (base matvec unamortised).
  - nonlinearity: the exact base+delta identity BREAKS. The contract
    'output == full recompute' holds only for the linear operator. Any
    real pipeline's nonlinear stages need recompute or approximation,
    which this experiment does not attempt.
